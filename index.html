About

Finance analyst who builds the numbers and explains them.

I'm a Financial Analyst on PNC's Operations Finance team. I run monthly budget-vs-actual variance across a $50M expense base and write the commentary that goes to business leaders.

Most of my week is SQL and Excel: pulling and reconciling the data, building the forecast, then explaining what moved and why. I redesigned our reporting templates so the monthly close takes 3 days instead of 5, and a review of vendor spend and processing fees turned up $450,000 in potential annual savings.

Before PNC I worked in finance and analytics roles in India at upGrad, BYJU'S and BSNL, building tuition revenue forecasts, departmental budgets, pricing models and leadership MIS. At BSNL I reconciled network usage against billing and found $1.2 million in potential annual revenue leakage.

I use generative AI to draft SQL and first-pass variance notes, and every number gets checked against source records before it goes out.
